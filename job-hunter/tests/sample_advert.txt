Supply Chain Graduate Programme 2027
Example Manufacturing (Pty) Ltd — Durban, KwaZulu-Natal

Our 18-month Supply Chain Graduate Programme rotates graduates through procurement, demand planning,
warehousing and distribution.

Requirements:
- BCom or degree in Logistics / Supply Chain Management (NQF 7)
- Advanced Excel (pivot tables); SAP exposure an advantage; Power BI advantageous
- Understanding of inventory management, stock control and procurement processes
- Knowledge of demand planning and forecasting
- Strong communication, problem solving and attention to detail
- Code 08 driver's licence

Duties:
- Support inventory control, cycle count and stock variance investigations
- Assist with supplier management and purchase order follow-up
- Prepare KPI reporting dashboards for the supply chain team
- Participate in continuous improvement (lean) projects
